# Logbook

This is yours. It is what makes the course *your* course.

**What to write down:** what you did not understand the first time · the compiler or JVM error you could not read · the thing you believed from your other language that is different in Java · what surprised you.

**Why it matters:** in two weeks you will not remember what was hard for you. And what was hard for you is exactly what needs reviewing.
